HOW IT WORKS

Conflict-aware reservation & no-show release

  1. Type the RFID UID printed on the card; the verified owner name fills automatically.
  2. The website checks the shared Firebase calendar and rejects overlaps.
  3. Arrive within 15 minutes of the booked start time or the slot is released automatically.
  4. At the station, tap the same card. Its UID must match the confirmed booking.
A successful RFID check-in during the 15-minute arrival window keeps the booking active. Unregistered cards cannot create a booking.
